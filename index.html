TCSHS Through the Years

Foundation and Development

Toledo City Science High School was established to provide science-oriented secondary education for learners in Toledo City and nearby communities.

Academic Growth

The school developed an academic environment emphasizing science, mathematics, research, and other areas of learning.

Student Development

As the school developed, students became involved in academic competitions, research activities, organizations, sports, and cultural programs.

TCSHS Today

TCSHS continues to provide students with opportunities to pursue academic excellence while developing leadership, creativity, scientific inquiry, and service to the community.

Core Responsibilities & Initiatives of the SSLG

  • Student Advocacy: Representing student interests, organizing student-led assemblies, and providing a platform for feedback on school policies or facilities.
  • Campus Mobilization: Spearheading active student participation in essential annual school activities such as the Brigada Eskwela (school maintenance and preparation).
  • Safety & Readiness Seminars: Collaborating with localized units to host Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (DRRM) workshops and managing student protocols during (National Simultaneous Earthquake Drills/NSED).
  • Leadership & Event Coordination: Designing and executing programs for student enrichment, academic mixers, and wellness initiatives that align with the rigorous expectations of the Special Science curriculum.

Structure & Elections

The organization is managed by an elected executive board of students (including a President, Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer) who undergo formal campaign and election cycles during the preceding school year. The SSLG operates under the guidance of Ma'am Rezie Kim Rivera, the SSLG's adviser and works in close coordination with the office of the school principal.

Youth for Environment in Schools Organization

The YES-O is the premier student-led environmental organization at TCSHS. It consolidates all ecology-related efforts under a single banner to cultivate a green-conscious campus.

  • Core Mandate: Established under DepEd Order No. 72, s. 2003, it serves as the official venue for student action toward safeguarding, promoting, and conserving the environment.

    • Barkada Kontra Bisyo

    The BKD is a peer-based organization focused on advocacy, substance abuse prevention, and promoting healthy student lifestyles.

    • Core Mandate: Institutionalized nationwide through the Dangerous Drugs Board (DDB) and DepEd, the organization leverages the Filipino concept of "barkada" (peer group) to foster a positive, supportive, and drug-free learning environment.
    • Key Activities: BKD focuses on preventive drug education, mental health awareness, and the productive use of time. They counteract the appeal of risky behaviors by driving alternative, constructive student engagements.
